# CampusFix Problem Statement 1. Problem

University facilities problems such as broken computers, projectors, laboratory lights, water leaks and faulty classroom doors are often reported informally. This makes it difficult to know which problems are unresolved, which are urgent, who is responsible for fixing them and how long requests have been waiting.

 2. Proposed Solution

CampusFix is a system designed to provide a centralized way for students and staff to report university facilities problems. The system will record each request, allow it to be prioritized and assigned to maintenance staff, track its progress and close the request once the problem has been resolved.

 3. Stakeholders

- Students
- University staff
- Maintenance staff
- University management
- System administrator

 4. Main Objective

The main objective of CampusFix is to provide an organized process for managing maintenance requests from the initial report to resolution and closure.

 5. Expected Benefits

CampusFix will help the university keep proper records of maintenance problems, track their progress, identify urgent requests and monitor how maintenance requests are handled.
